# AI in Design and Construction Course Portfolio

This repository contains my course portfolio website for **AI in Design and Construction**. The website presents weekly AI news analyses, reflections on emerging AI tools, and examples of how artificial intelligence can be applied in architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) contexts.

## Website Pages

- **Home** – introduces the course portfolio and its purpose.
- **About Me** – provides a brief background about my academic and professional interests.
- **AI News Analysis** – includes weekly AI-related news summaries, article links, prompts used, and reflections on relevance to AEC.
- **AI in AEC Use Cases** – presents class assignments and projects exploring practical AI applications, including AI-powered takeoff, mini-agent reviews, book review GPT use, and a custom Construction RFI Analyst.
